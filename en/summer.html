Summer at Ylläs Kajo

Bright nights and quiet trails

In summer Ylläs is peaceful and nature is right outside. There's plenty of availability for shorter breaks too.

Mountain biking

The Ylläs bike park was voted Finland's best in 2024 and 2025. Trails start almost from the door, and bikes stay safe overnight in the lockable storage.

  • Gondola and bike park about 1 km away
  • Singletrack and gravel routes around Ylläs
  • Bike rentals in Ylläsjärvi and Äkäslompolo
Visit Ylläs Bike Park →

Hiking & fells

The trails, summits and clear lakes of Pallas-Yllästunturi National Park are a short trip away. In midsummer the sun doesn't set at all.

  • Day hikes to the Ylläs summit and nearby fells
  • Trail running and family-friendly walks
  • Swimming and paddling on Lake Ylläsjärvi

Summer evenings at the cabin

Heat up the sauna, grill on the terrace and enjoy the bright night. The peaceful yard is perfect for long summer evenings.

  • Private terrace and charcoal grill
  • Private sauna whenever you like
  • Pets welcome

Ruska – autumn colours

In September the fells glow red and gold. Ruska is one of the most beautiful times in Lapland for hiking and picking berries and mushrooms.

  • Autumn hikes in the national park
  • Berry and mushroom picking
  • First northern lights as early as August–September

Work from the fells

The loft has two fully equipped desks. Work during the day and hit the trail in the evening – longer summer stays are easy to arrange.

  • Two desks with monitors
  • Fast WiFi
  • Ask about weekly rates
