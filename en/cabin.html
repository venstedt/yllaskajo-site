The cabin

Around 67 m² of cosy space for six: sauna, fireplace, a loft and everything you need for a holiday.

Spaces & amenities

Equipped as if for ourselves

Cleaning is included, so your holiday starts the moment you arrive.

Sleeping

  • Downstairs bedroom: double bed
  • Loft: 2 single beds and a sofa bed
  • Up to 6 guests in total

Kitchen & dining

  • Fully equipped kitchen
  • Induction hob, oven and dishwasher
  • Extendable dining table for 4–6
  • Coffee maker and kettle

Sauna & bathroom

  • Bathroom with private sauna
  • Separate toilet
  • Washing machine
  • Drying cabinet by the entrance

Living & remote work

  • Fireplace and underfloor heating
  • Google TV / Chromecast and fast WiFi
  • Two desks on the loft with monitors, keyboards and mice

Yard & storage

  • Private terrace and charcoal grill
  • Large lockable storage for bikes and snowmobiles
  • Ski waxing stand and two sledges
  • Woodshed

Arrival & parking

  • Self check-in with a key box
  • Free parking for two cars
  • EV charging €10 per 12 h
